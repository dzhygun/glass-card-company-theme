Privacy & Cookies Policy

1. General information

We respect the privacy of visitors to our website. This Privacy & Cookie Policy explains what data is collected when you visit our website, how it is processed, and which cookies may be used.

2. Data controller

The operator and owner of this website is:

Vladyslav Zolotukhin
vladyslav@zolotukhin.ch

3. Services we use

This website is hosted and delivered via Cloudflare to ensure security, performance, and availability.
Cloudflare acts as a data processor on our behalf.

When you visit the website, Cloudflare automatically processes technical connection data, including:

  • IP address and country

  • Browser information (User-Agent)

  • Requested URL

  • Timestamp and response status

  • Security-related metadata (e.g., Bot Fight Mode verification).

4. Cookies

Our website does not use any advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

We use Cloudflare as a security, performance, and access provider.
Cloudflare may set strictly necessary cookies to ensure secure delivery of content, protect the website from malicious traffic, manage load balancing, handle access authentication, and maintain service availability.
These cookies are not used for advertising or user tracking.
Some cookies are set only if specific Cloudflare features are enabled (e.g. Waiting Room, Load Balancing, Access).

Strictly necessary cookies do not require consent under the ePrivacy Directive, but must be disclosed in Privacy & Cookie Policy.

5. Analytics

We use Cloudflare Account and Web Analytics, which collects aggregated traffic statistics.
No cookies or persistent identifiers are used for this purpose.
Only technical connection data is processed to ensure security and performance.

6. Data transfers

Technical connection data may be processed on Cloudflare servers worldwide, including the United States.
Data transfers are based on Standard Contractual Clauses (SCC) to ensure adequate protection under GDPR.

7. Legal basis for processing

The processing of technical connection data described in this policy is based on:

  • Article 6(1)(f) of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) — the legitimate interest of the website operator in providing a secure, stable, and performant website.

  • Article 31 of the Federal Act on Data Protection (Switzerland) (nFADP / revDSG) — processing is necessary to safeguard overriding legitimate interests in ensuring the security, availability, and reliability of the website.

8. Your rights

Under applicable data protection laws, you may have the right to:

  • request information about the processing of your personal data,

  • request access to personal data held about you,

  • request deletion or restriction of processing,

  • object to certain types of processing.

This website does not collect or store personal data directly.
All technical data (such as IP addresses) are processed exclusively by Cloudflare as our hosting and security provider. We do not have access to or control over this data.

If you wish to exercise your data protection rights related to this processing, you may contact Cloudflare directly:
